# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Peers and connections in the software-testing / tech community who arrive from a link
on LinkedIn, GitHub, or a Thai QA community, plus the occasional recruiter. They browse
on desktop or mobile, usually for a minute or two, to answer one question: *"Who is
Panthawit 'Boo' Chumthong, and is he the real deal as a QA engineer?"* The job to be
done is to quickly grasp his range (manual through automation across many tools), trust
that the skills are genuine, and find a frictionless way to connect or follow.

## Product Purpose

A personal-brand portfolio that establishes Boo as a credible, well-rounded Software
Tester / QA professional and makes connecting effortless. It is not a lead-gen funnel or
a job-application gate; it is the canonical "this is me and my work" hub that every other
profile (LinkedIn, GitHub, resume PDF) points to. Success: a visitor leaves with a clear
sense of his breadth and craft, and at least the easiest path to reach out (email,
LinkedIn, GitHub) feels obvious. The portfolio doubles as living proof of the discipline:
the site itself is the work sample.

## Brand Personality

Friendly but professional ("เป็นกันเองแต่สุภาพ"). A warm, first-person storyteller who is
enthusiastic about the craft yet precise and credible. Three words: **approachable,
precise, genuine.** The copy should feel like meeting a real person who genuinely enjoys
finding bugs ("I find bugs before the users do"), not like reading a corporate brochure.
Bilingual by nature: Thai and English both feel native, never bolted on.

## Anti-references

- **Generic resume-template / CV-builder sites.** No off-the-shelf "online CV" look that
  could belong to anyone.
- **Loud SaaS gradient landing pages.** No fluorescent gradients, gradient text, hero-metric
  template, or hype-deck energy.
- **Stiff, cold corporate sites.** No enterprise stock-photo formality, no buzzword voice,
  nothing that reads as a vendor pitch.

## Design Principles

- **Show the work, not adjectives.** Real artifacts (README case studies, test GIFs,
  certificates) carry credibility. Demonstrate range; don't claim it.
- **Practice what you preach.** A QA's own site must be polished, responsive, accessible,
  and bug-free. Craft IS the proof of the discipline; sloppiness here undermines the pitch.
- **Human first, never corporate.** First-person storytelling voice; visiting should feel
  like meeting Boo, warm and specific, not like reading a brochure.
- **Bilingual as a first-class citizen.** Thai and English are peers, not translations
  bolted on, so both the local network and international peers feel addressed.
- **Substance over spectacle.** Restraint beats flash. Keep the linear-minimalist calm so
  the content stays the hero; resist trendy template and gradient clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** (chosen as the sensible default for a public personal site):
- Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold text ≥ 3:1, including placeholder and muted copy.
- Visible focus states and full keyboard operability for every interactive piece: project
  cards, the README/terminal modal, the certificate image viewer, language (EN/TH) and
  theme toggles, and pagination.
- Meaningful `alt` text on certificate and profile images; semantic headings/landmarks.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for hover/scale transitions and any reveal motion.
- Bilingual EN/TH content; comfortable line lengths and legible type at all breakpoints.
